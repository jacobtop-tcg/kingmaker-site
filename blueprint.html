Kingmaker Venture blueprint v2
2026-08-31 · Copenhagen

The executive search house for the technical win.

Kingmaker is the invite-only register and search house for the engineers who decide enterprise deals: Sales Engineers, Solutions Consultants and Architects, and Forward Deployed Engineers. The elite are represented and placed on their own terms, and companies come here when the technical win cannot be left to chance. Tagline: The deal is won before the seller speaks.

1. The category insight

Every enterprise deal lives or dies in the technical evaluation, yet the people who win those evaluations have no institution. Presales talent is recruited through three broken channels: generalist sales recruiters running SE searches with AE playbooks (Betts, RevPilots), sub-scale contingency boutiques with no product, data, or community (Harvey Thomas, 3Search, J. Patrick), and in-house LinkedIn sourcing fighting 10 to 15 recruiter-spam messages a week. The largest community in the craft, PreSales Collective at 50,000 members, deliberately monetizes membership and job posts, not elite search. The Nordics have zero SE-specialist recruiters at all.

Meanwhile the roles are repricing violently upward: US median SE OTE is $205k (senior $239k, AI/ML $263k, cybersecurity $255k), and Forward Deployed Engineer postings grew roughly 800% in 2025 with no specialist recruiter serving the category. The talent pool of roughly 300k to 500k people globally is too small for venture-scale marketplaces, which is exactly why Hired ($150M+ raised, brand killed June 2024) and Triplebyte (dead March 2023) failed here, and exactly the right size for a high-touch category king: a few thousand elite profiles cover most of the world's premium demand.

The winning architecture combines four proven playbooks never combined in this category: Bravado (community-first economics), Toptal and A.Team (vetting scarcity as prestige), Levels.fyi and Ravio (verified compensation data as moat), and Korn Ferry (retained-search fee gravity), pointed at a vacant number-one seat.

2. The name

Kingmaker states the quiet truth of enterprise software out loud: the account executive wears the crown, but the engineer decides who wins the kingdom. A cynical Principal SE reads "represented by Kingmaker" as overdue recognition; a CRO signing a "Kingmaker search" knows in one word what tier of weapon they are buying. Spellable after one hearing, unoccupied in the executive-search category, and it extends into an empire: the Kingmaker Register, Kingmaker Fellows, The Kingmaker Report.

Chosen from a five-direction naming sprint of 40+ collision-checked candidates. Runners-up: Onsight, Ravn, Cadenza, Valkyr. Required before launch spend: Nice class 35 trademark clearance (EU, UK, US) and domain acquisition. Fallback name: Ravn.

3. The four moats, sequenced honestly

Supply lock-up

The Register

Standing relationships with the world's top few thousand customer-facing engineers, built out from Europe: peer-nominated, evidence vetted, free forever, majority not job-seeking. A place on the Register outlasts every job switch, and placed engineers become hiring managers who buy searches. Early prestige comes from who is in (a named Founding Jury, a consented roll of names), never from fabricated statistics.

Vetting IP

The Proof

SE skill is uniquely observable, and every serious SE hire already ends in a mock discovery-and-demo panel. Kingmaker codifies the five dimensions companies already test. Vetting runs on evidence and references, never mandatory auditions; the recorded Showcase is candidate-owned (the Fellow tier), and the rubric is monetized employer-side as a structured interview kit.

Data

The Report

No credible European SE compensation source exists. Comp capture is embedded in every vetting interview from engineer one, at zero cost. Publish a "Snapshot" at n of about 100 with published methodology; claim benchmark authority only at n of 400+ offer-verified datapoints across five countries. Explore a Ravio or Figures partnership before they ship an SE cut.

The standard

The Score

Ten scored dimensions of SE-organization health. A scored diagnostic, not a revenue gate: every introduction discloses the organization's score and gaps to the candidate; only the public endorsed-employer badge is gated at 80+. The Michelin position on both sides, without refusing cold-start revenue.

6. Go to market: one city, one revenue line, then compound

Now

Phase 0: The Pool (North-Star mode)

The operating mode the two founders start in together: the full institution site is the public face, with pool-first honesty written into it (zero counters, the first-fifty cadence stated where the Register forms); operations run quiet and on the side, one or two exceptional people a week. One Register, two internal tracks: a senior track (deep enterprise craft, natural jury and fractional-bench supply, most motivated for decade-long representation) and the fde/core track. No company-side outbound until three triggers fire: 25+ Represented, 3+ named jurors, one inventory-matched lighthouse req. Intake runs through the detailed form, one conversation at a time.

After activation

Copenhagen beachhead

Founding Jury of 4-6 respected SE leaders first (published names, fixed honorarium, no equity, 24-month terms; no Fellow or jury language publishes before three are named), then the Founding 100 via founder-led vetting interviews, 8-10 a week. The cohort closes at one hundred names with permanent privileges: the founding roll, nomination rights into the first Fellow jury pool, every Report first. Brand site live. Sign 2-3 lighthouse searches only when the Register verifiably holds 3+ plausible candidates: sell from inventory, not into it. Targets: 30 on the Register, 60%+ passive, one offer out.

Activation +3-6 mo

Proof and first data asset

SaaStr AI London for UK and EU demand; Copenhagen and Stockholm dinners; about 100 on the Register. Publish the European SE Compensation Snapshot (n of about 100, methodology published). Close 3-5 placements, €75-150k.

Activation +6-18 mo

Productize and moat

Register directory and search workflow replace Airtable; Talent Partner pilot; chapter captains in Berlin, London, Amsterdam after placement 10; benchmark v2 as give-to-get data product; fractional bench live; FDE practice for EU scale-ups; 100% outcome logging.

Beyond

Category king

US-remote retained practice from months 12-24; US ground presence only after €2M run-rate. Path to €5-10M as the global category king of customer-facing engineering talent.

9. The red-team verdict, recorded

"The category insight is genuinely good: customer-facing engineering talent is a real, unowned, repricing niche, and the register-plus-data-plus-standard architecture is the right end state. Fix the prestige cold start, the Showcase, and the launch commercial terms, convert the health check from gate to scored diagnostic, cut year one to 6-8 placements in one city with honest cash modeling, and the plan becomes a fundable, bootstrap-viable path to a €1.5-2M elite boutique by year three, with a real option on the category-king position."

All named fixes are incorporated above. The ambition remains number one in the world; the sequencing now earns it.
