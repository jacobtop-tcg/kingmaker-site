How a search runs

Every enterprise deal has a kingmaker.

The account executive carries the quota and collects the crown. But walk any deal back to the moment it tipped: a discovery call that reframed the problem, a demo that held a hostile room, a proof of concept that ended the argument. The person who did that was an engineer. The buyer remembers their name.

Today those engineers are recruited by volume messages from people who could not pass their own discovery call. The best of them are not on job boards. They are employed, quota-crushed, and quietly unaware of what they are now worth.

Kingmaker is their institution. We keep the register of the engineers who decide deals, most of whom are not looking. We verify the wins, we score the employers, and every search we take is exclusive. We take one only when the Register plausibly holds the match, or we can name the path to it, and we tell you which the same day.

Representation, not recruitment.

The Register holds the engineers we represent: free for life, mostly not looking, entered by nomination or evidence, never by signup. When the right mandate exists, we already know each other. Five commitments, each with the mechanism that enforces it:

1

Never spammed

One named agent, and today that agent is a founder who has carried the POC himself. Approaches only above your stated bar: compensation floor, title, segment, travel ceiling. One follow-up, then silence is respected.

2

Your employer learns nothing from us

Anonymous dossiers under a k-anonymity rule, per-company blocklists, an NDA before any reveal, and double opt-in on every introduction. You approve the exact text before anything is sent.

3

You always know your market price

Every datapoint we hold for your market, labeled honestly: document-verified or attested, never blended. Refreshed quarterly, with negotiation counsel from former SE leaders when an offer lands.

4

The Register means something

Entered by peer nomination and evidence, with a consented roll of names. We never publish rejections, and we never publish inflated statistics.

5

We work for your career, not this quarter's fee

You see everything sent about you and the same data the company sees. No fee-driven steering, with a named remedy. When the internal counteroffer is genuinely better, we say so.

We score the employers, too.

Elite engineers do not leave good jobs for bad organizations. So every hiring organization is scored against the Kingmaker Standard: ten dimensions of presales health, from the ratio of sellers to engineers to whether SE leadership has ever carried the bag itself.

The score is not a gate. It is the truth, disclosed. Every introduction a candidate receives carries the organization's score and its gaps. Eighty and above earns the endorsed employer mark. Below sixty gets an honest conversation about what to fix first.

Your score is confidential. It is shown only to candidates inside your own search, never published, never shared with anyone else; only the 80-plus endorsed mark ever appears in public. Scoring happens inside the brief call, from evidence you already hold.

No other channel shows an engineer this before they spend an evening interviewing.
